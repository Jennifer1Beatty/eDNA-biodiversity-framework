Starting Point

Choose the policy or dataset type that is relevant to you.

Method Choice

Environmental DNA methods range in their selectivity and their readiness for policy integration.

Sample Processing

Thorough documentation and metadata is essential for successful eDNA studies that adhere to FAIRe policies. Consistency in sample protocols is necessary for comparable studies.

Data Analysis

How sequences are processed is as important as how they are generated, as these decisions impact the ecological interpretation of eDNA data. Again, consistency in processing and proper documentation is necessary to ensure comparability.

Biodiversity Indicators

The types of biodiversity data that each method can provide.

Final Product

Target Taxa

Define the target taxonomic group (e.g., fish, amphibians, macroinvertebrates). Current standard frameworks prioritize optimizing primer sets specifically matching local regional reference registers.

Method Suggestion

Based on the target taxa, choose between targeted species detection or broad-scale community profiling. Sampling considerations that impact biodiversity assessment include: sampling depth, volume, daily timing.

Policy Initiative

Align the biological survey requirements with regulatory frameworks, local management directives, or environmental protection goals.

Metabarcoding

Utilized for broad biodiversity assessments across multiple trophic levels simultaneously using high-throughput sequencing panels. Short read sequencing is well established and long-read sequencing is developing. Organisms targeted based on the primers used.

ddPCR / qPCR

Best for targeted species monitoring (e.g., invasive pests or highly endangered target species) where quantitative sensitivity is paramount.

Metagenomics

Shotgun sequencing applications used to capture whole environmental genomes without targeted PCR bias.

Metatranscriptomics

Shotgun sequencing applications used to capture whole environmental transcripts without targeted PCR bias.

PCR Primers

Metabarcoding primers target entire groups: Fish (COI, 16S, 12S), plankton (18S- microbial eukaryotes; 16S- prokaryotes), fungi, virus, mammal, birds.

qPCR/ddPCR primers are more targeted and need to be carefully designed to ensure accuracy.

Details regarding primer selection validation, in-silico testing profiles, and minimizing amplification bias.

Sequencing Platforms

Operational instructions for benchmarking standard Illumina platforms vs. long-read options like Oxford Nanopore based on the survey scope.

DNA Extraction

Standardized extraction protocols using commercial kits, custom magnetic bead cleanups, and clean-lab configurations to isolate quality yields.

Library Preparation

SOPs for index ligation, cleanups, absolute quantification, and pooling controls to prevent cross-contamination.

Reference Databases

Direct taxonomic link outs to NCBI, BOLD, or specialized regional reference indices used for sequence assignment validation.

Bioinformatics Pipeline

Standard data processing flows including quality filtering, denoising (ASVs vs. OTUs), chimera checks, and taxonomy assignment.

Biodiversity Metrics

Methods for computing Alpha diversity indices, Beta community variations, and multi-metric indices based on sequence data counts.

EVs (Essential Variables)

Mapping eDNA outputs directly to national or global Essential Biodiversity Variables (EBVs) frameworks.
